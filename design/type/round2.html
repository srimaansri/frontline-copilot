Type candidates, round two

Round one put eight whole sets on the screens. This round takes the three jobs apart: the display face against the mark, the text face that is read aloud, and the data face for references and numbers. Everything else is held still: the text beside each display face is Atkinson Hyperlegible Next.

1. Display face: the name, the titles, the headline

At the three display sizes in use: 22 (the name in the top bar), 30 (view titles), 46 (the headline), and the name at 64 beside the mark.

2. Text face: what the agent reads aloud

18px with the highlighter, then the small sizes: a 12.5px cue, a 14px detail, a 15.5px button, a status word. The last line is the confusable characters.

3. Data face: references, placeholders, counts

Each beside Atkinson Hyperlegible Next at 18px, as in an answer.
