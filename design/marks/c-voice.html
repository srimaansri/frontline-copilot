Mark candidates, family C: the voice in the agent’s ear

Four marks for Frontline Copilot from one idea: the copilot is the calm voice that tells the agent what to say. Each is drawn on a 32-unit grid, with a redrawn 16-unit version used at 24 and 16 pixels. Every size below is shown at true pixel size.

Colour rule used throughout: marker only ever sits on cobalt or navy, because on paper it has no contrast. On dark grounds the cobalt body turns to paper so the mark keeps its weight.

Construction
32-unit grid, solid shapes only. A 26 × 26 rounded square, outer radius 8, hollowed to a wall of 6. The visor slot is 14 high with inner radius 4 and runs out through the right side. The upper arm ends in the 45° SIM cut (legs of 6); the lower arm ends in a quarter-circle of the wall’s own width. Eyes are 3 × 6 pills, 7 apart, centred in the slot.
The aha
The face looks out through the gap, so in a lockup it looks at its own name. Read as a letter it is a C, should the product become Cue. Read as an outline it is the SIM card: three round corners and one cut.

Ranking

  1. Aside. Holds its shape from 256 to 16, is a character without a mouth or a smile, carries the SIM cut, and survives a rename to Cue.

  2. Cue card. The most on-identity of the four, because the highlighter is the face. Below 32 it starts to read as a file or memory-card icon, and it needs all three colours to tell its story.

  3. One line. The warmest, and the only one that draws the voice in the ear. It is a line, so it is light at small sizes, it has no SIM cut, and it can read as the human agent, not the robot.

  4. Offcut. The tidiest idea and the weakest presence. A speech bubble with two eyes is the chatbot category’s default, and the cut corner alone does not lift it clear. Its eyes are dots, not bars, to keep it away from Twitch’s glyph.

Aside wins because it is the only one that is a robot face, the SIM-card outline and a letter-ready form at once, in two flat shapes that hold at 16 pixels. Its weakness is that a blocky C is not ours alone (Coda’s mark is the nearest neighbour), so it must never appear without its eyes and its cut terminal, and on paper those eyes are ink: the highlighter only shows on cobalt and navy.
