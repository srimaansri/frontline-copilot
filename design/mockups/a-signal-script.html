Direction A

Signal and Script

A support agent works from a script, so the copilot's answer is set like one: the facts to read aloud are run over with a highlighter, and anything private is struck out with a redaction bar. Whatever the copilot wrote sits on a card with one corner cut like a SIM, so shape alone says who is speaking. Four signal bars fill through its four checks while it works, the way a phone finds its network.

Copilot

The late-payment fee is $5. Dial 611 from your Northline phone.

The one bold element The highlighter. It is the only yellow-green in the product, and it only ever sits on words the agent says out loud.

Shape and motif rules

$5
Highlighter

Means: say this out loud. Bold facts in an answer (a price, a number to dial, the hand-off reference). Only inside text the copilot wrote. Never on buttons, headings, status or selection.

PERSON_1
Redaction bar

Means: a personal detail was here and was removed. A solid bar with the placeholder in mono. Only on placeholders; no other running text is set light on ink.

SIM chamfer

Means: the copilot wrote this. One corner, top right, cut at 45 degrees, outlined in the edge colour. Only on the answer card, the summary card, the pending answer and the mark. People's words, buttons and inputs keep square 3px corners.

Signal bars

Means: the copilot's own state. One bar per stage every request passes: privacy, request check, tool, answer check. They fill as stages finish; all four steady in What's running. Never a rating, never decoration.

Status shapes

Means: circle OK, square notice, triangle warning, octagon blocked. Colour and the word repeat what the shape already says. Used on steps, on call status and in the row of stamps that sums up a run before its detail.

1
Citation chip

Means: this claim has a source. The same outlined square numeral sits after the claim and in front of the source, so the eye can match them. Numbers follow citation order.

Ink fill

Means: you are here. The current view and the active filter are filled with ink. A selected list row takes the cobalt tint with an ink outline, so status colours inside it stay legible.

Cobalt

Means: you can press this. Send, links, citation chips, and the bars while live. Never used for status, and never as a background wash.
