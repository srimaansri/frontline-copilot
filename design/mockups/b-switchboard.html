Identity sheet

Tokens, faces, icons and the rules that keep each device meaning one thing.
Direction B

Switchboard

Switchboard treats every question as a call placed on a line. The copilot's work is drawn as the route that call travelled, station by station, with a lamp at each one that stays hollow when all is well and lights when something needs the agent. When a request is beyond the copilot, the line visibly branches and is patched through to a person, the way an operator would have done it.

The one bold element: the cord, a vermilion route line with a lamp at every station, whose 45° branch patches a request through to a person. Everything else on the board is enamel grey and stays quiet.

Colour

Neutrals take the grey-green of exchange frame enamel and the green-black of bakelite, so the cord is the only warm line on the board. Lamp colours are never used for brand.

Shape and motif rules

Each device means one thing. If the thing is not true, the device is not drawn.
  • Line with stations

    A sequence that happened, in order. The copilot's steps, a call's turns, a summary's events. Never a divider or decoration.

  • Cord colour

    The one live route on a screen and the controls that put something on it. Route, send, citations, links, the selected jack. Never a status.

  • 45° branch

    The request left the copilot for a person. Hand-offs only, ending on a filled person plug that names who has it.

  • Lamp

    A status, always round. Hollow with a tick: nothing needs you. Lit core: notice. Solid: warning or blocked. Always with its word; colour never works alone.

  • Jack

    A small ring is a point you can connect to; filled means connected. Example cards, call rows, a citation and its source.

  • Plate

    Capitals on 2 px corners name a fixed thing. Stages, tools, speakers, counts. A plate is never clickable.

  • Key

    4 px corners and a 1.5 px outline mean you can operate it. Pressed is an inverse fill. Buttons, filters, the view and theme switches, source rows.

  • Mono chip

    Text that stands in for something else or is read one character at a time. Placeholders in notice blue, call ids, references, addresses.

  • Numbers

    Only where order is real. Steps to give a customer, a citation and its source, these artboards.

  • Dashed outline

    An annotation on this board. It does not exist in the product.
